# Obtaining Real ERISA Disclosure Documents for Testing

This guide shows you how to obtain authentic 408(b)(2) fee disclosures and plan documents for testing COLA II's detection capabilities.

---

## Quick Start (15 Minutes)

**For the Graves Competition demo**, you can get test documents immediately:

### Option 1: Major Provider Sample Disclosures (FASTEST)

These are publicly available and contain real fee structures:

**Fidelity Workplace Services:**
- Visit: https://workplace.fidelity.com/
- Navigate: Resources → Plan Sponsors → Fee Disclosure Tools
- Download: Sample 408(b)(2) Service Provider Fee Disclosure
- Contains: Revenue sharing, recordkeeping fees, fund expense ratios

**Vanguard:**
- Visit: https://institutional.vanguard.com/investments/solutions/plan-participant-services
- Look for: "Service Provider Disclosure" or "Plan Fee Information"
- Download PDF samples
- Contains: Clean institutional pricing (good for comparison)

**TIAA:**
- Visit: https://www.tiaa.org/public/plansponsors
- Search: "408(b)(2) disclosure"
- Download: Service Provider Disclosure samples
- Contains: Multiple service provider structures

**Estimated Time:** 15-30 minutes
**Cost:** Free
**Usefulness:** High - real fee structures without sensitive participant data

## Option 2: Public University 403(b) Plans (FREE via FOIA)

Many state universities publicly post plan documents:

**Already Public:**
- **University of California System:** Search "UC 403(b) fee disclosure"
- **SUNY System:** Public records requests fulfilled quickly
- **Florida State University System:** Often posted on treasurer websites
- **Texas A&M System:** Public institution disclosures

**How to Find:**
1. Google: `[University name] 403(b) fee disclosure site:.edu`
2. Check university treasurer/benefits pages
3. If not posted, email: `benefits@[university].edu`
4. Public universities must comply with open records laws

**Example Request Email:**
```
Subject: Public Records Request - 403(b) Plan Fee Disclosure

Dear Benefits Office,

Under [State] open records law, I request a copy of:
1. Most recent 408(b)(2) service provider fee disclosure
2. Most recent participant fee disclosure

These are public documents for a state university retirement plan.

Please provide in PDF format. Thank you.
```

**Estimated Time:** 2-7 days for email response
**Cost:** Free
**Usefulness:** Very high - real plans, real fee structures, publicly available

## Option 3: DOL Form 5500 Filings (PUBLIC RECORD)

Large plans (>100 participants) file annually with the Department of Labor:

**EFAST2 Portal:**
- Visit: https://www.efast.dol.gov/portal/app/disseminate
- Search by: Company name, EIN, or plan name
- Download: Form 5500 + Schedule C (service provider fees)
- Many attach 408(b)(2) disclosures as exhibits

**Best Searches:**
- Fortune 500 company 401(k) plans
- Large public companies (10,000+ participants)
- Look for Schedule C attachments

**Example Companies to Search:**
- General Electric 401(k)
- Boeing Retirement Savings Plan
- Walmart 401(k)
- Amazon Corporate LLC 401(k)

**Estimated Time:** 30-60 minutes searching
**Cost:** Free
**Usefulness:** Medium-High (not all attach full disclosures)

## Option 4: Supreme Court Case Briefs (FREE)

For cases that reached appellate/Supreme Court, briefs often excerpt disclosure language:

**Cornell/Cunningham Case:**
- Visit: https://www.supremecourt.gov/
- Search: Cunningham v. Cornell University (2024)
- Download: Petitioner's Brief, Respondent's Brief
- Contains: Excerpts of actual TIAA/Fidelity disclosures

**Other Useful Cases:**
- Harris v. Amgen (included sample Fidelity disclosures)
- Tussey v. ABB (exhibits included fee schedules)
- Hughes v. Northwestern (complex multi-provider disclosures)

**Estimated Time:** 20-30 minutes
**Cost:** Free
**Usefulness:** High for validation, but excerpts only

## Option 5: PACER Litigation Documents (Requires Account)

For actual litigation exhibits, you can purchase documents from federal court records:

**PACER Setup:**
1. Sign up at: https://pacer.uscourts.gov/
2. Cost: $0.10 per page (capped at $3.00 per document)
3. Quarterly fee waived if <$30 usage
4. Budget: ~$15-25 for key documents

**Recommended Documents to Purchase:**

**Tussey v. ABB (E.D. Mo. Case 4:06-cv-04305):**
- Court Opinion (free - posted publicly)
- Exhibit A: Fidelity Service Agreement (~50 pages = $3)
- Exhibit B: Fee Schedule (~20 pages = $2)
- **Total: ~$5**

**Cunningham v. Cornell (N.D. Ill. Case 1:16-cv-8157):**
- Complaint with exhibits (~100 pages = $3)
- TIAA Contract excerpts (~40 pages = $3)
- **Total: ~$6**

**Harris v. Amgen (C.D. Cal. Case 2:07-cv-05442):**
- Settlement Agreement (~30 pages = $3)
- Disclosure Exhibits (~50 pages = $3)
- **Total: ~$6**

**Grand Total for 3 Cases: ~$17-20**

**Estimated Time:** 2-3 hours (including account setup + searching)
**Cost:** ~$20
**Usefulness:** Very high - actual litigated documents

## What to Look For in Documents

When you obtain disclosures, prioritize documents containing:

✅ **Fee Schedules:**
- Recordkeeping fees (per participant or % of assets)
- Transaction fees
- Distribution fees

✅ **Revenue Sharing:**
- 12b-1 fees
- Sub-TA fees
- Shareholder servicing fees
- "Revenue sharing" or "indirect compensation" sections

✅ **Investment Lineup:**
- Fund expense ratios
- Share class information (Class A vs. Institutional)
- Affiliated fund disclosures

✅ **Service Provider Info:**
- Compensation details
- Affiliate relationships
- Float income procedures

✅ **Obfuscation Red Flags:**
- "See other agreements..."
- "Available upon request..."
- Excessive footnotes
- Complex calculation formulas

## Legal & Ethical Notes

✅ **100% Legal to Use:**
- Provider sample/template disclosures
- Public university documents (open records)
- Court filings and exhibits (public record)
- Form 5500 filings (DOL public database)

⚠️ **Redact if Sharing:**
- Participant names
- Specific account balances
- SSNs or EINs (if present)

✅ **Best Practice:**
- Use documents for research/development only
- Don't publish actual participant data
- Provider samples and public records are safe to demo
