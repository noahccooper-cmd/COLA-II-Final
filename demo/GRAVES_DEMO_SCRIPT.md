> "Great question - let me finish the demo and I'll address that in Q&A."

## Q&A Preparation

**Expected Questions:**

**Q: "How accurate is the detection?"**
> "We've validated against actual court findings from Tussey, Cornell, and Harris cases - $90M+ in combined settlements. Our patterns directly match language that courts found violated ERISA."

**Q: "What's your revenue model?"**
> "SaaS subscription: $500/month for RIAs (10+ plans), $2,000/month for enterprise. We're targeting the 15,000 RIAs managing 401(k) plans - $90M TAM."

**Q: "What prevents someone from copying this?"**
> "The data gravity. A new entrant starts with zero benchmarks. We compound advantages with every document. After 1,000 documents analyzed, we have 1,000x more intelligence than a competitor at day one."

**Q: "Why would someone switch from their current tool?"**
> "Current tools are PDF parsers. We're an intelligence platform. After analyzing 50 plans, switching means losing 50 data points of benchmarking. Network effects create switching costs."

**Q: "How do you handle false positives?"**
> "Every finding includes confidence scores, court precedent citations, and LLM analysis. Users see WHY something was flagged, not just that it was. They can assess risk contextually."

**Q: "What's your competitive advantage?"**
> "Three layers: (1) Lawsuit-trained patterns that competitors don't have, (2) Data gravity from our benchmark database, (3) First-mover advantage in building the largest compliance dataset."

**Q: "How big is the market?"**
> "640,000 401(k) plans in the US. 15,000 RIAs manage multiple plans. Every plan must provide 408(b)(2) disclosures annually. We're targeting the professional market first - advisors and plan sponsors with 100+ participants."

**Q: "What's your customer acquisition strategy?"**
> "ERISA attorney partnerships, content marketing (we publish benchmark reports quarterly), and freemium tier (analyze 3 documents free, $500/month for unlimited + benchmarks)."
